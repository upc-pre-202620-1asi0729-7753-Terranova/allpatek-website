Milestone Payment Guarantee

Gradual release of escrow funds backed by direct field verification.

  1. Milestone 1

    Contract signing and lock of initial capital in secure escrow.

  2. Milestone 2

    Planting verification and release of funds for inputs.

  3. Milestone 3

    Growth inspection and real-time climate monitoring.

  4. Milestone 4

    Harvest validation and production quality approval.

  5. Milestone 5

    Final lot delivery and release of the remaining balance.
